# Privacy Policy — WastePlanner and Pro Waste Consultants

Last updated: 20 October 2026 · Version 2.0

## 1. About this policy

Pro Waste Consultants Pty Ltd ABN 88 693 080 349 (**PWC**, **we**, **us**) operates WastePlanner at wasteplanner.au and related subdomains (the **Platform**) and the Pro Waste Consultants website at prowaste.au (the **Website**). We handle personal information in accordance with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles. This policy applies to the Platform, the Website, and enquiries and fee proposal requests made to PWC. It explains what we collect, why, who we share it with, and your choices.

## 2. Information we collect

- **Account information:** name, email, phone number, company name and role.
- **Project data:** site addresses, development details, calculations and layouts you create, and documents and drawings you upload. These may contain personal information about other people, such as names in title blocks or owner details.
- **Usage data:** how you use the Platform, device and browser type, IP address, and log data.
- **Marketing and attribution data:** how you found us, such as campaign (UTM) parameters and ad click identifiers.
- **Register-interest and form details:** if you request a fee proposal, register interest, request a council, or email yourself calculator results, the name, email, phone number, company, role, project details, drawings and other inputs you provide.
- **Communications:** emails, calls and messages you exchange with us.
- **Payment information:** processed by Stripe. We do not store full card details.

## 3. How we collect it

We collect information directly from you, automatically through cookies and similar technologies, and from third parties such as Stripe, advertising platforms, or your organisation when it adds you to an account.

## 4. How we use it

We use personal information to:

- provide, maintain and support the Platform;
- respond to enquiries and prepare fee proposals;
- process payments;
- process uploaded documents using AI features (section 5);
- send service communications, such as account, security and billing notices;
- send marketing communications, which you can opt out of at any time;
- understand how the Platform is used and improve it;
- create aggregated, de-identified benchmarks;
- protect the security of the Platform and prevent misuse;
- comply with our legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information.

## 5. AI processing

When you use AI features, such as the compliance checker or plan upload, extracted text and page images from your documents are sent to Anthropic, PBC (United States) through its API for processing. Data is transmitted over HTTPS.

Under Anthropic's commercial terms, data sent through the API is not used to train AI models. Anthropic may retain it for a limited period in line with its data retention policies, including for trust and safety purposes.

By using AI features, you confirm you are authorised to submit the relevant documents for processing by a third party.

## 6. Cookies and analytics

We use essential cookies to keep you signed in and the Platform secure. We also use analytics and advertising technologies, such as Google Ads conversion tracking, to understand how people find and use the Platform. You can control cookies through your browser settings. Blocking essential cookies may stop the Platform working.

## 7. Storage and security

Account data and project records are stored in Supabase in the AWS Sydney (ap-southeast-2) region. We protect information with encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS), row-level database security, secure authentication through Supabase Auth, and access controls. Payments are processed by PCI-DSS compliant Stripe. No system is completely secure. If an eligible data breach occurs, we will notify affected individuals and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner as required by law.

## 9. Overseas disclosure

Some providers in section 8 store or process information outside Australia, mainly in the United States. We take reasonable steps to ensure they handle it consistently with the Australian Privacy Principles.

## 10. Retention

- **Account data:** kept while your account is open (free or paid) and for 12 months after it closes.
- **Project data:** kept for 24 months after you last access it, then deleted or de-identified.
- **Billing and tax records:** kept for the period required by tax and corporations law.
- **Backups:** deleted data may remain in backups for up to 30 days.
- **Register-interest details:** kept until we have told you the Platform is open, then deleted unless you create an account.

You can delete your account at any time from Account Settings ("Delete my account"), or ask us to delete your data earlier by emailing info@prowaste.au, subject to records we must keep by law. When an account is deleted, its personal projects and files are deleted. Projects that belong to an organisation stay with that organisation. Projects that PWC was engaged on are kept for our professional records.

## 11. Your rights

You may request access to, or correction of, the personal information we hold about you, or ask us to delete it. You can opt out of marketing communications at any time using the unsubscribe link or by contacting us. We will respond within 30 days.

## 12. Complaints

If you have a privacy complaint, contact us first and we will respond within 30 days. If you are not satisfied, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

## 13. Age

The Platform is intended for professional use by people aged 18 or over.

## 14. Changes

We may update this policy. We will notify you by email of material changes.
